Interaction, elevation & colour discipline
Hover is a fill, never a lift

On white, shadow-on-hover reads as smudge. Rows and cards tint to Canvas; navy surfaces lighten one step.

One focus ring, everywhere

2px Link navy with 2px offset, set globally on :focus-visible. The old dark theme forgave weak focus; white does not.

Elevation is scarce

Shadows only for things that float — modals, sheets, menus, toasts. Inline cards use a hairline. In dark mode shadows vanish, so elevation becomes a #1B2A4D border.

Two hues, plus danger

Navy and Link carry every state. Success and warning are icon plus navy text — no green, no amber. Danger is the one exception.

Mixed-script rhythm
